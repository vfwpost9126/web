How It Works

Local assistance reviewed by Post 9126.

VFW Post 9126 maintains a Veteran Relief Fund to provide limited financial assistance to veterans experiencing financial hardship.

1

Submit a request

Complete the Veteran Relief Request form with your contact information, veteran verification, household and financial information, and details about the assistance requested.

2

Provide supporting information

The request form asks for proof of veteran status and supporting documentation such as an estimate or invoice when applicable.

3

Committee review

The Veteran Relief Committee reviews requests and may approve assistance of up to $300.

4

Payment goes to the party owed

If approved, assistance is paid directly to the vendor, company, landlord, utility provider, creditor, or other party to whom payment is due. Funds are not paid directly to the applicant.

Important Details

Before you apply

  • The committee may approve up to $300.
  • Requests above $300 must be presented to the Post membership for consideration at the next regular Post meeting.
  • Assistance is subject to review and the availability of Veteran Relief funds.
  • Submitting a request does not guarantee approval or funding.

Need immediate help?

Veteran Relief requests are not an emergency service and may not be reviewed immediately. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. Veterans in a mental health crisis, or anyone concerned about a Veteran, can call 988 and press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line.
