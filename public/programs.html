Programs

Connection looks different for everyone.

That’s why Post 9126 is building multiple ways for veterans and the community to connect, learn, serve and belong.

Music & Veterans

Guitars for Vets Oklahoma

Veterans can receive ten weeks of free one-on-one instruction through Operation Unplugged, Operation High Voltage or Operation Bass Camp. In-person students can use a loaner instrument during the course, and graduates receive a guitar and accessories.

Every Wednesday

Community Wednesday

The Post is open Wednesdays from noon to 5 PM for a community-welcoming afternoon of connection and activities. Depending on the week, that can include Dungeons & Dragons, board games, movie nights and more.

You do not have to be a veteran to participate.

Come as you are.

Community Wednesday is intentionally low-pressure. Stop in, meet people, check out what is happening and stay as long as you like.

Guitars for Vets Alumni Program

Operation Encore

Operation Encore is part of Guitars for Vets and gives program alumni a continuing place to return, play together and stay connected after graduation.

Sundays · 12–2 PM

Operation Encore is hosted at VFW Post 9126 for Guitars for Vets alumni.
