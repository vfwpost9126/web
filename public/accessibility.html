Accessibility

Accessibility Statement.

VFW Post 9126 wants our website to be useful and accessible to veterans, families, and members of our community.

Our Commitment

We want everyone to be able to use this site.

VFW Post 9126 is committed to improving the accessibility and usability of vfw9126.org. We work to use clear content, keyboard-friendly navigation, meaningful image descriptions, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and other common accessibility practices.

If you encounter an accessibility barrier

If any part of this website is difficult to access or use, please contact us and tell us what page or feature caused the problem. We will make a reasonable effort to provide the information in another format or help you access the content.

Third-party services

Some parts of the site connect to services operated by other organizations, including Google Calendar, Zeffy, Jotform, Formspree, Facebook, Instagram, and Discord. Those services may have their own accessibility features and limitations. If a third-party service creates a barrier, contact us and we will try to help you find another way to access the same Post information or service.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. As the Post adds new programs, events, photos, and website features, we will continue reviewing the site and correcting issues we identify.
