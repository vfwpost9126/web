Start Here

You do not have to figure it out alone.

VFW Post 9126 helps veterans connect with resources for VA disability claims, appeals, benefits questions and related assistance.

Because every situation is different, we work to connect veterans with the resource that best fits their specific needs.

If you need help understanding where to start, navigating a claim, or finding someone who can assist with your situation, contact the Post and let us know what kind of help you are looking for.

VA Disability Claims

Need help finding the right place to start or someone who can assist with a disability claim.

Appeals & Claim Questions

Have questions about an existing claim, decision, appeal, or what your next step may be.

Benefits Questions

Need help finding an appropriate resource for VA benefits or related veteran-service questions.

Connection to Resources

We will use the information you provide to help point you toward a resource that fits your situation.

What to Send Us

Just enough to understand what you need.

Use the website contact form to briefly describe the type of help you are looking for. We can follow up from there.

Protect your privacy.

Do not submit Social Security numbers, VA claim numbers, medical records, passwords, or other sensitive personal information through the website contact form.

Contact Post 9126
Our Role

Helping you find the right resource.

Post 9126's role is to help connect veterans with an appropriate resource based on the situation they describe. The person or organization a veteran is referred to may vary depending on the type of assistance needed.
