Gaming at the Post

A community that happens to game.

Post 9126 Gaming gives veterans and community members a place to connect through PC and online gaming, tabletop games, fantasy sports, shared interests, and friendly competition.

Join us in person or connect through the Post 9126 Gaming Discord to find players, plan games, ask for help, and keep up with tournaments and community activities.

Ways We Play

More than one kind of gaming.

Whether you play on a PC, around a table, or through a fantasy league, there is a place to jump in.

PC + ONLINE

PC Gaming

Talk games, builds, releases, multiplayer sessions, hardware, and everything else PC gaming.

  • Looking for Group and teammate matching
  • Game suggestions and community-night ideas
  • Tech help for PCs, networking, controllers and peripherals
  • PC tournaments and organized competition
TABLETOP

Tabletop Gaming

Roll dice, build decks, pull up a chair, and find people who enjoy the same games you do.

  • Dungeons & Dragons campaigns, one-shots and DM discussion
  • Magic: The Gathering, including Commander
  • Board games, recommendations and game-night planning
  • Tabletop looking-for-group and tournaments
FANTASY SPORTS

Fantasy Sports

Our gaming community also connects around fantasy leagues, friendly competition and season-long discussion.

  • Fantasy football
  • Fantasy baseball
  • Fantasy basketball
  • League announcements, recaps, drafts, trades and lineup talk
