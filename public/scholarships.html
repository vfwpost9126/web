VFW Scholarships

VFW Post 9126 proudly participates in Patriot’s Pen and Voice of Democracy, giving local students the opportunity to share what service, citizenship and America mean to them.

2026–2027 Theme

“What a Veteran Taught Me About America”

Post 9126 submission deadline: October 31, 2026

Grades 6–8

Patriot’s Pen

Written essay competition

Eligibility
Students in grades 6–8 enrolled in a public, private, parochial school, or home-study program in the U.S. and its territories.
Essay
300–400 words (±5 words), written in English on the annual theme.
Submission rules
No color or graphics. No personal identifiers in the essay. The entry form is the cover sheet.
National award
$5,000 national first-place grand prize, with more than $55,000 awarded in national prizes and scholarships each year.
Deadline
Submit to VFW Post 9126 by October 31, 2026.
Entry Form & Details View Flyer
Grades 9–12

Voice of Democracy

Audio-essay competition

Eligibility
Students in grades 9–12 enrolled in a public, private, parochial school, or home-study program in the U.S. and its territories.
Audio essay
3–5 minutes (±5 seconds) on the annual theme.
Entry includes
Recorded audio essay, typed essay and entry form.
National award
$35,000 national first-place college scholarship, with more than $2 million in educational scholarships and incentives awarded every year.
Deadline
Submit to VFW Post 9126 by October 31, 2026.

Youth & Civic Engagement

Investing in the next generation.

Scholarships are one part of how Post 9126 encourages young people to engage with service, citizenship and their community.

The annual Patriot’s Pen and Voice of Democracy programs give students a chance to reflect, write and be heard.
