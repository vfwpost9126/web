Membership

Your service didn’t end your ability to serve.

The VFW connects eligible veterans through advocacy, camaraderie and continued service to fellow veterans and the community.

Why Post 9126?

  • Connect with veterans who understand the experiences that shaped you.
  • Take part in community service and outreach.
  • Help shape programs for the next generation of veterans.
  • Join social, music, gaming and community activities.
  • Be part of the VFW’s continued advocacy for veterans and military families.

Am I eligible?

VFW membership eligibility is based on qualifying military service. The easiest way to confirm eligibility is through VFW National or by talking with Post 9126.
